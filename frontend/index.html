Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy outlines how NexCommerce ("the Platform"), developed for Course 25CS1302E (Database Systems & Distributed Backend Development) at KL University, collects, processes, and protects user data across its polyglot storage layer.

1. Data Collection & Purpose

The platform collects and stores user information exclusively for authentication, order processing, and multi-warehouse inventory allocation:

  • Identity & Credentials: Name, academic email address, bcrypt-hashed passwords, and RBAC role assignments.
  • Transactional Data: Orders, items, shipping destinations, and transactional amounts recorded immutably in PostgreSQL.
  • Telemetry & Session Logs: Temporary cache keys, reservation tokens, and audit transaction records.

2. Security & Storage Architecture

All sensitive credentials are encrypted using industry-standard bcrypt hashing. Sessions are secured via JSON Web Tokens (JWT) signed with HS256. Relational transaction integrity is enforced via PostgreSQL ACID properties and row-level locks.

3. Data Retention & Access

Data stored in PostgreSQL and MongoDB is retained strictly for evaluation and academic testing purposes. Users may review order histories and audit trails directly through the verified database inspector.

Terms and Conditions

Last Updated: January 1, 2026

By accessing or using the NexCommerce Platform, you agree to comply with and be bound by the following academic and operational terms.

1. Platform Scope & Academic Usage

NexCommerce is an engineering software project built for Course 25CS1302E - DBS-DBD at KL University (Off-Campus Aziz Nagar). All catalog items, prices, and simulated transactions are intended for system evaluation, benchmarking, and database demonstration.

2. Transactional Consistency & Concurrency

All checkout transactions execute under strict ACID guarantees on PostgreSQL (klhdb). Attempted orders exceeding warehouse availability are subject to automatic transaction rollback without partial database writes.

3. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Administrative privileges (catalog creation, stock adjustment, audit inspection) require authenticated JWT credentials with ADMIN or WAREHOUSE_MANAGER role permissions.

4. Intellectual Property

The system architecture, database schemas, and codebase are authored by the engineering project team under the Department of Computer Science & Engineering, KL University.
